The Game

How to Play

A party game where anonymity is the point — write, bluff, and vote your way to glory.

🕵️
Live Now
Anonymous Stories
A prompt appears — everyone writes something in response, anonymously. Then the whole room votes on who they think wrote each one. The sneakier your style, the harder you are to catch.
1
Host picks a pack & starts the room
The host creates a room, picks a game pack that sets the vibe, and shares the room code with everyone.
🎲 Safe Chaos 🔥 After Hours 💀 Unhinged 💌 Wholesome 🎬 Hollywood 🌀 Twisted Reality
2
A prompt drops — everyone writes
Each round, a prompt appears for everyone. You write whatever feels right — go obvious, go subtle, go completely off the wall. The only rule is nobody can see who wrote what. Your job is to blend in or throw people off.
Anonymous Timed
3
The room votes — who wrote this?
All responses are shown one by one — no names attached. Everyone votes on who they think wrote it. No hints, no conferring — just your gut and whatever you know about your friends.
🗳 Live Vote
4
Points & the big reveal
Authors earn points for every wrong guess. Guessers earn points for sniffing out the right author. At the end of each round, the truth comes out.
Writer earns
+800 per wrong guess

Repeat for all rounds → winner takes the crown
